## The Golden Rules of the Final Protocol
* **Zero Fatigue:** The warm-up should never push you to failure or make your muscles burn[cite: 410]. If your movement slows down, stop and rest, because you are training neurological speed, not endurance[cite: 411].
* [cite_start]**Rest Thoroughly:** Take a full 2 to 3 minutes of rest after finishing this entire routine before starting your actual plyometric or campus workout[cite: 412]. [cite_start]Your central nervous system needs to be completely recovered to generate maximum power safely[cite: 413].
