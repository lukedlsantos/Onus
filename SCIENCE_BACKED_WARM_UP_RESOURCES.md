## 1. The RAMP Protocol Framework for Climbing

Research in sports medicine strongly warns against static stretching prior to high-performance output (which reduces muscle stiffness and rate of force development). Instead, modern training adopts the **RAMP** protocol:

### **R**aise (5–10 Minutes)
*   **Objective:** Increase heart rate, core temperature, dynamic respiration, and joint fluid elasticity.
*   **Application:** Systemic unweighted movements: high knees, skipping, jumping jacks, or light jumping rope.

### **A**ctivate (5–10 Minutes)
*   **Objective:** Wake up stabilizer muscle chains (rotator cuff, scapular retractors, core, glutes, and forearm finger flexors).
*   **Application:** Band pull-aparts, banded external rotations, tendon glides (for fingers), and glute bridges.

### **M**obilize (5–10 Minutes)
*   **Objective:** Move key joint hubs (hips, thoracic spine, ankles, shoulders) through dynamic ranges of motion.
*   **Application:** Leg swings, arm circles, deep lunges with thoracic twists (World's Greatest Stretch), and 90/90 hip switches.

### **P**otentiate (10–15 Minutes)
*   **Objective:** Progressively ramp up force output to peak session intensities, priming the neuromuscular system.
*   **Application:** Overcoming isometrics on a tension block (progressive percentages of Maximum Voluntary Contraction) and easy movement on the wall.

## 2. The Hiroshi Chiba Tore Biomechanical Prep

Hiroshi Chiba’s training methodology revolves around **Four-Legged, Two-Axis Movement (四足二軸運動理論)**—coordinating the scapular engine and pelvic engine contralateral slings (e.g., right shoulder to left hip) to maximize on-wall movement efficiency. 

His warm-up phase focuses on:
1.  **Movement Screening:** The overhead towel squat to check scapula-pelvis coordination.
2.  **Targeted Release:** Utilizing a tennis ball/high-density ball to release pec minor, rhomboid, and latissimus restriction zones.
3.  **Contralateral Coordination:** Specific diagonal movements (Staggered Squat & Elbow Circle, Supine Hand-to-Toe Touch).
4.  **Daily Autoregulated Neural Screening:** Checking MVC percentage drop-offs via isolated pulls against a force gauge.
