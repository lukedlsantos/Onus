# Project Onus - Month 1: Capacity & Base Phase Curriculum

This file serves as the definitive programming blueprint for the 4-week Capacity & Base macrocycle block. It implements a 3-hour on-wall container, a progressive loading structure, and an aggressive 1% Progress Hook counter-decay strategy.

---

## Part 1: Global Training Blocks & Standard Containers

### 1. Standardized 40-Minute Prep Container (Used on Days 1, 3, and 5)
* **Off-Wall Movement Prep [30 Minutes]:**
    * **Neck & Shoulders:** 3 direction neck half-circles (slow), 15 passes thoracic spine foam rolling, 10 reps resistance band shoulder dislocates, and 12 reps scapular wall slides.
    * **Core, Chest & Back:** 10 cat-cow fluid transitions, 8 bird-dogs per side, 45-second static doorway deep chest stretch, and 45-second active child's pose with lateral latch reach (left and right).
    * **Hips & Ankle Mobility:** 60-second deep goblet squat hold with a light weight, 10 dynamic 90-90 hip switches, and 12 reps dynamic ankle wall-touches per leg to optimize deep foot flagging and heel/toe hooks.
    * **Fingerboard Structural Loading:** 3 sets × 10-second unweighted static isometric hangs on a 20mm edge. Rest 60 seconds between hangs. Alternate between open-hand grip and half-crimp positions to safely lubricate the pulley tissues and progressive load tendons without generating fatigue.
* **On-Wall Progressive Ramp [10 Minutes]:** Continuous, slow bouldering movement entirely on vertical or moderate slab terrain. Keep heart rate low and avoid tracking any forearm pump. Focus exclusively on crisp hold engagement and fluid joint tracking.

### 2. Microcycle Pacing Variables (Week-by-Week Engine Progression)
To automate the training block progression via the codebase, adjust the global variables for the Tier 2 Core Drivers across the 4-week block using these inputs:
* **WEEK 1 (Baseline Engine):** Day 1 & Day 5 On-Wall Rest: **90 seconds**. Target: **20 problems completed**. Day 3 Density: **4 blocks of 4 problems**.
* **WEEK 2 (Progressive Overload):** Day 1 & Day 5 On-Wall Rest: **75 seconds**. Target: **22 problems completed**. Day 3 Density: **4 blocks of 5 problems**.
* **WEEK 3 (Peak Strain Window):** Day 1 & Day 5 On-Wall Rest: **60 seconds**. Target: **25 problems completed**. Day 3 Density: **5 blocks of 4 problems**.
* **WEEK 4 (Automated Deload Recovery):** Global volume drop of 40–50%. Tier 2 duration drops to exactly **45 minutes** at an easy, fluid pace. Rest constraints are lifted. Day 6 gym intensity scales down by 50%.

## WEEK 1: BASELINE ENGINE BLOCK

### Week 1 - Day 1: Low-Neural / High-Volume Capacity (3 Hours)
* **Tier 1: Warm-Up & Prep [40 Mins]:** Execute Standard 40-Minute Prep Container exactly.
* **Tier 2: Core Driver — High Mileage Volume [90 Mins]:**
    * *Protocol:* Execute 20 unique vertical to gentle-slab boulder problems at low-to-moderate physical intensity.
    * *Pacing:* Rest strictly limited to 90 seconds between problems to drive vascular capillarization.
* **Tier 3: Progress Hook — Technique Base & Core Tension [40 Mins]:**
    * *Protocol:* Movement library saturation. Complete repetitive paths on flash-grade terrain. Focus on zero foot readjustments, silent feet placements, precision hip tracking, and strict flagging extension.
* **Tier 4: Care, Advanced Core & Lower-Body Plyometrics [10 Mins]:**
    * *Climbing Core & Upper Push:* 2 sets × max time hanging static L-sits. 2 sets × 15 deep diamond push-ups. 2 sets × 8 deep bench dips.
    * *Wrist, Shoulder & Lower Plyos:* 2 sets × 15 reverse wrist curls with a light dumbbell. 1 min deep dead-hang shoulder decompression. 3 sets × 6 explosive box jumps with soft, controlled landing mechanics to activate leg drive.

### Week 1 - Day 2: Physical Therapy Core & Recovery (2 Hours)
* **Aerobic Influx Flush [30 Mins]:** Continuous low-intensity, steady-state nasal breathing jog.
* **Climbing-Specific PT Core Container [40 Mins]:**
    * *Exercises:* 3 sets × 45-second anti-rotational cable Pallof presses per side. 3 sets × 12 reps single-arm kettlebell farmer's carries per side to lock down lateral stability chains. 3 sets × 10 slow, high-tension Swiss-ball rollouts.
* **Deep Shoulder, Spine & Mobility Matrix [50 Mins]:**
    * *Exercises:* 3 sets × 15 seconds passive sleeper stretch for posterior rotator cuff tracking. 3 sets × 10 reps prone standard Y-T-W arm raises on floor. 3 sets × 45 seconds deep puppy pose shoulder/lat stretch. 3 sets × 60 seconds cross-legged spine twists.

### Week 1 - Day 3: Structural Capacity & Base Off-Wall Stability (3 Hours)
* **Tier 1: Warm-Up & Prep [40 Mins]:** Execute Standard 40-Minute Prep Container exactly.
* **Tier 2: Core Driver — On-Wall Density Ladders [90 Mins]:**
    * *Protocol:* Style variety density ladders. Complete 4 distinct blocks of 4 different moderate boulder problems back-to-back across sloper, crimp, and compression angle layouts. Rest 3 minutes between major blocks.
* **Tier 3: Progress Hook — Highly Optimal Off-Wall Foundation [40 Mins]:**
    * *Protocol:* Structural alignment overload. 3 sets × 8 reps strict overhead dumbbell press. 3 sets × 8 reps heavy kettlebell goblet squats. 3 sets × 12 reps cable face-pulls with high contraction hold to counter active climbing shoulder slouch.
* **Tier 4: Postural Care & Plyometric Booster [10 Mins]:**
    * *Protocol:* 5 minutes targeted deep forearm rolling and cross-friction finger tissue massage. 3 sets × 6 explosive broad jumps emphasizing complete hip extension.

### Week 1 - Day 4: Asynchronous Recovery Selector
* *Code Instruction: Render selector giving the athlete two paths based on baseline readiness.*
* **Option A (Active Flush):** Re-execute Day 2's deep mobility and climbing PT core container.
* **Option B (Passive Rest):** Zero physical training stress. Complete off-load to repair skin calluses and finger pulley networks.

### Week 1 - Day 5: Density Volume & Technical Capacity (3 Hours)
* **Tier 1: Warm-Up & Prep [40 Mins]:** Execute Standard 40-Minute Prep Container exactly.
* **Tier 2: Core Driver — Medium-Volume Coordination [90 Mins]:**
    * *Protocol:* Complete 10–12 mid-tier problems focusing on coordination steps, dynamic drop-knees, and directional tracking on sub-optimal foot placements. Rest 2 minutes between attempts.
* **Tier 3: Progress Hook — Under-Load Technical Repeats [40 Mins]:**
    * *Protocol:* Re-climb flash-grade problems under climbing fatigue while actively working through a localized forearm pump to reinforce technical precision when exhausted.
* **Tier 4: Structural Care [10 Mins]:** 3 sets × 15 repetitions wrist extensor band extensions, 45 seconds doorway chest wall stretches, and 2 minutes passive edge dead-hangs.

### Week 1 - Day 6: Climbing-Specific PT Gym Session (90 Mins)
* **Warm-Up & Stabilization [15 Mins]:** 2 sets × 15 band pull-aparts, shoulder internal/external rotations, and active glute bridges.
* **Shoulder Girdle & Pulling Alignment [35 Mins]:**
    * *Exercises:* 3 sets × 10 reps of eccentric pull-ups (4-second lowering phase) and 3 sets × 12 reps cable face-pulls to reinforce posterior structural tracks.
* **Pushing & Anterior Posture Corrections [30 Mins]:**
    * *Exercises:* 3 sets × 8 reps kettlebell floor press (shoulder joint capsule safety) and 3 sets × 8 reps single-arm dumbbell overhead presses.
* **Core Integration [10 Mins]:** 3 sets × 10 reps hanging leg raises focusing on slow eccentric control.

### Week 1 - Day 7: Absolute Rest Day
* *Protocol:* Zero physical training stressors. Complete off-load to dissipate central nervous system fatigue entirely.
