# Project Onus - Month 3: Max Strength Phase Curriculum

This file serves as the definitive programming blueprint for the 4-week Max Strength macrocycle block. It implements a 3-hour on-wall container, maximum motor-unit recruitment protocols, active scapular centration, and overcoming isometric finger conditioning.

---

## Part 1: Global Training Blocks & Standard Containers

### 1. Standardized 40-Minute Prep Container (Used on Days 1, 3, and 5)
*   **Off-Wall Movement Prep [30 Minutes]:**
    *   **Neck & Spine:** 3 slow direction neck half-circles, 15 passes thoracic spine foam rolling, and 10 dynamic Spine Wave transitions.
    *   **Scapular & Shoulder Girdle:** 12 passes resistance band shoulder dislocates, 10 reps foam roller Serratus wall slides, and 8 reps prone Y-T-W-L raises (unweighted) to centrate the shoulder joints.
    *   **Hips, Pelvic Core & Lower Chain:** 10 dynamic 90-90 hip switches, 8 dynamic hip CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations) per side, and 12 reps resisted dorsiflexion ankle wall-touches per leg to maximize flagging extension and high step rock-overs.
*   **Autoregulated Neural Screening [5 Minutes]:**
    *   Perform 2 submaximal warm-up pulls against a Tindeq portable strain gauge in half-crimp position at 50% and 75% MVC (Maximum Voluntary Contraction).
    *   If daily force output is down by **>10%** from baseline, immediately scale back the Core Driver volume by 50% to prevent connective pulley injury.
*   **On-Wall Progressive Ramp [10 Minutes]:** Easy, controlled bouldering on steep system boards ($30\text{–}45^\circ$) utilizing large footholds and positive grips. Focus exclusively on pelvic-shoulder contralateral chains. Zero forearm pump.

### 2. Microcycle Pacing Variables
To automate the training block progression via the codebase, adjust the global variables for the Tier 2 Core Drivers across the 4-week block:
*   **WEEK 1 (Baseline Profile Block):** Day 1 Limit Board: **8 climbs completed** (RPE 9, 4m rests). Day 3 Compound Strength: **4 sets $\times$ 5 reps at baseline max load**.
*   **WEEK 2 (Accumulation Block):** Day 1 Limit Board: **10 climbs completed** (RPE 9.5, 4m rests). Day 3 Compound Strength: **Advance external loads by 5%** (4 sets $\times$ 5 reps).
*   **WEEK 3 (Peak Strain Matrix Block):** Day 1 Limit Board: **12 climbs completed** (RPE 9.5–10, 5m rests). Day 3 Compound Strength: **Peak load configuration** (4 sets $\times$ 5 reps).
*   **WEEK 4 (Automated Deload Window):** Automated volume reduction of 50%. Core Driver duration drops to exactly **45 minutes** at an easy, fluid pace on vertical/slab faces. Fingerboard overcoming pulls are completely eliminated.

## WEEK 1: BASELINE PROFILE BLOCK

### Week 1 - Day 1: High-Neural / Absolute Limit Bouldering (3 Hours)
*   **Warm-Up & Prep [40 Mins]:** Standard Prep Container with neural screening.
*   **Core Driver: Limit Bouldering Projecting [90 Mins]:**
    *   *Protocol:* High-intensity projecting on steep system boards ($35\text{–}45^\circ$).
    *   *Execution:* Complete 8 distinct baseline limit board climbs. Focus on 1–3 hyper-difficult moves, driving maximum inward pressure from feet and counteracting rotational torque (barndooring) via pelvic-shoulder linkage.
    *   *Pacing:* Rest strictly 4 minutes between burns to preserve ATP-PC replenishment.
*   **Progress Hook: Capacity Preservation [40 Mins]:**
    *   *Protocol:* Defending forearm vascular capillary density.
    *   *Execution:* 2 sets $\times$ 15 minutes continuous vertical ARC climbing with hover cues (3s pause before latching). Rest 5 minutes between sets.
*   **Care & Restoration: Chiba Tore Core & Antagonist Stabilization [10 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* 2 sets $\times$ 10 reps Supine Hand-to-Toe Alternating Touch (diagonal slings) and 2 sets $\times$ 8 reps/side Single-Hand Elevated Scapular Push-Up (serratus/lower trap motor control).

### Week 1 - Day 2: Physical Therapy Core & Recovery (2 Hours)
*   **Aerobic Influx Flush [30 Mins]:** Continuous nasal-breathing steady-state jog or cycle ($<140$ BPM).
*   **Climbing-Specific PT Core Container [40 Mins]:**
    *   *Exercises:* 3 sets $\times$ 10 reps of standing cable Pallof presses, side planks (45s/side), Swiss-ball rollouts, and Seated Contra-Lateral Leg Raises (hip flexor conditioning).
*   **Deep Shoulder, Spine & Mobility Matrix [50 Mins]:**
    *   *Exercises:* 5-minute thoracic extensions over a foam roller, passive sleeper stretches, prone dumbbell Y-T-W-L raises, and lat/capsule banded flossing.

### Week 1 - Day 3: Compound Max Strength & Max Fingerboard (3 Hours)
*   **Warm-Up & Prep [40 Mins]:** Standard Prep Container with neural screening.
*   **Core Driver: Heavy Compound Strength Overload [90 Mins]:**
    *   *Protocol:* Maximum neuromuscular force compound resistance training.
    *   *Execution:* 4 sets $\times$ 5 reps of heavy weighted pull-ups (progressive load targeting, add +20–40 lbs), heavy dumbbell chest floor presses, barbell rows, and Heels-Elevated Weighted Squats.
    *   *Constraints:* Rest 3–4 minutes between sets.
*   **Progress Hook: Max Edge Overcoming Pulls [40 Mins]:**
    *   *Protocol:* Autoregulated active maximum half-crimp pulls against Tindeq gauge.
    *   *Execution:* 5 repetitions of a 7-second active maximum-effort concentric pull against the fixed block at 95% MVC. Rest 3 minutes between pulls to maximize recruitment.
*   **Care & Restoration: Antagonist Balance & Explosive Plyometrics [10 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* Forearm extensor bands, wrist roller rolls, and **Depth Jumps to Max Vertical Vector** (3 sets $\times$ 5 reps; focus on quick ground contact and explosive launch).

### Week 1 - Day 4: Asynchronous Recovery Selector
*   **Option A (Active Flush):** Re-execute Day 2's deep mobility and core container.
*   **Option B (Passive Rest):** Complete off-load. Zero physical stress to maximize skin repair and clear finger pulley inflammation.

### Week 1 - Day 5: High-Velocity Board Work & Power (3 Hours)
*   **Warm-Up & Prep [40 Mins]:** Standard Prep Container with neural screening.
*   **Core Driver: Contact Strength & Dynamic Launches [90 Mins]:**
    *   *Protocol:* Dynamic board jumps and contact power catches.
    *   *Execution:* Complete 8 distinct dynamic problems on steep angles ($30\text{–}40^\circ$). Focus on pelvic launch and straight-arm skeletal stacking on contact catch.
    *   *Constraints:* Rest exactly 3 minutes between burns to maintain explosive velocity.
*   **Progress Hook: Under-Pump Technical Repeats [40 Mins]:**
    *   *Protocol:* Coordination execution under progressive physical fatigue.
    *   *Execution:* Complete 6 distinct flash-grade problems on overhanging terrain ($15\text{–}30^\circ$). Maintain a flush pelvis and silent foot placements.
*   **Care & Restoration: Forearm Decompression & Extensors [10 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* Rice bucket hand drills (3 Mins), pen rolling (2 Mins), forearm flexor self-massage (5 Mins).

### Week 1 - Day 6: Climbing-Specific PT Gym Session (90 Mins)
*   **Warm-Up & Stabilization [15 Mins]:** Band pull-aparts and basic glute activation.
*   **Rotator Cuff & Scapular Base Grid [25 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* Dumbbell prone Y-T-W-L raises (3 sets $\times$ 10 reps) paired with foam roller Serratus wall slides (3 sets $\times$ 10 reps).
*   **Lower Body posterior sling [30 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* Deficit Kettlebell Reverse Lunges (3 sets $\times$ 8 reps/leg) paired with Kettlebell Floating RDLs (3 sets $\times$ 10 reps/leg).
*   **Anti-Extension Trunk Stability [20 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* 3 sets $\times$ 12 reps of deadbugs and ab-wheel rollouts.

### Week 1 - Day 7: Absolute Reset Day
*   *Protocol:* Full physiological reset. No training parameters tracked.

## WEEK 4: AUTOMATED DELOAD WINDOW

### Week 4 - Day 1: Low-Neural / Active Recovery Flush (3 Hours)
*   **Warm-Up & Prep [40 Mins]:** Prep container using light, slow patterns. Fingerboard limited to zero-load passive hanging.
*   **Core Driver: Low-Intensity Active Recovery [90 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* Complete exactly 10 problems on simple vertical faces or slabs. Avoid crimps, edges, or dynamic lunges.
    *   *Constraints:* Rest 3 minutes between completions to flush joint inflammation.
*   **Progress Hook: Mobility Regeneration Track [40 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* Climb easy paths focusing on high-steps, deep drop-knees, and pelvic proximity.
*   **Care & Restoration: Gentle Posture Care [10 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* 2 sets of: push-ups (8 reps), bodyweight dips (5 reps), passive deadhangs (30s).

### Week 4 - Day 2: Deep Mobility & Parasympathetic Sync (2 Hours)
*   *Exercises:* 2 sets slow bird-dogs, deadbugs, and low-load standing cable presses.

### Week 4 - Day 3: Structural Maintenance & Joint Off-Load (3 Hours)
*   **Warm-Up & Prep [40 Mins]:** Deload prep container.
*   **Core Driver: Reduced Density Work [90 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* Complete 2 style blocks containing 3 easy vertical problems. Rest 4 minutes.
*   **Progress Hook: Joint Articulation & Alignment [40 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* Single-leg balance stands, quad stretches, passive shoulder circles, and wall slides.
*   **Care & Restoration: Light Antagonist Care [10 Mins]:**
    *   *Execution:* Unloaded wrist circles and forearm foam rolling.

### Week 4 - Day 4: Absolute Rest Day
*   *Protocol:* Complete structural reset. No active recovery.
