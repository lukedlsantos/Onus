# Phase Curriculum Ledger: Months 1 to 6 Workout Programs

This document contains the complete production-ready markdown dataset for the 6-Month Training Plan, outlining the structural progression across all phases.

---

## 6-Month Block-Conjugate Program Structure & Phase Mapping

### Month 1: Capacity & Base Phase
* **Primary System Trait:** Vascular capillarization and structural connective tissue tolerance.
* **Microcycle Schedule:** 2 Volume Sessions, 1 Technique Session, 1 Recovery Session per week.
* **Session Structure:** `[Tier 1: Pulse] -> [Tier 2: Volume / High Mileage (70%)] -> [Tier 3: Technique Focus (30%)] -> [Tier 4: Care]`
* **Core Driver Protocol:** Baseline volume building. 15–20 vertical to slight-spray board problems completed at low-to-moderate intensity with short, efficient rest intervals.
* **Maintenance Hook Protocol:** Movement refinement. Strict technical execution drills such as quiet feet, precise hip tracking, and continuous body-tension repeats.

### Month 2: Basic Strength Phase
* **Primary System Trait:** General progressive overload and baseline recruitment.
* **Microcycle Schedule:** 1 Off-Wall Strength, 1 Medium-Volume, 1 Technique, 1 Recovery Session per week.
* **Session Structure:** `[Tier 1: Pulse] -> [Tier 2: Basic Off-Wall & Board Strength (70%)] -> [Tier 3: Capacity Maintenance (30%)] -> [Tier 4: Care]`
* **Core Driver Protocol:** Introduction to board syntax. Controlled climbing sequences on moderate angles alongside baseline off-wall compound resistance movements.
* **Maintenance Hook Protocol:** Forearm plumbing defense. Inject a continuous, low-intensity 15-minute volume block right at the end of the session to keep Month 1's capillary pathways active.

### Month 3: Max Strength Phase
* **Primary System Trait:** Neuromuscular maximum force output ($F_{\text{max}}$).
* **Microcycle Schedule:** 2 Limit Bouldering Sessions, 1 Off-Wall Strength Session, 1 Recovery Session per week.
* **Session Structure:** `[Tier 1: Pulse] -> [Tier 2: Limit Bouldering / Max Hangs (70%)] -> [Tier 3: Strength Maintenance (30%)] -> [Tier 4: Care]`
* **Core Driver Protocol:** Neurological max out. 1–3 move maximal cruxes on steep system boards. Rests are strictly maintained at 3–5 minutes between high-intent, high-quality execution burns.
* **Maintenance Hook Protocol:** Basic strength retention. Heavy isometric core loading or controlled locks performed only during low-volume deload weeks to eliminate junk volume spikes.

### Month 4: Power Phase
* **Primary System Trait:** Rate of Force Development (RFD) and kinetic coordination velocity.
* **Microcycle Schedule:** 2 Dynamic Power Board Sessions, 1 Speed/Coordination Session, 1 Recovery Session per week.
* **Session Structure:** `[Tier 1: Pulse] -> [Tier 2: Explosive / Coordination Board (70%)] -> [Tier 3: Max Force Maintenance (30%)] -> [Tier 4: Care]`
* **Core Driver Protocol:** High-velocity execution. Dynamic coordination moves, deadpoints, and contact strength catches performed with complete recovery between individual attempts.
* **Maintenance Hook Protocol:** Force ceiling defense. 2 high-intensity, heavy finger recruitment pulls executed at the end of the warm-up before dynamic climbing to protect raw finger strength from decaying.

### Month 5: Power Endurance Phase
* **Primary System Trait:** Anaerobic lactic tolerance and complex link efficiency under fatigue.
* **Microcycle Schedule:** 2 Power Endurance Sessions, 1 Technique Under Pump Session, 1 Recovery Session per week.
* **Session Structure:** `[Tier 1: Pulse] -> [Tier 2: Heavy Neural Triggers (30%)] -> [Tier 3: Lactic Capacity / Circuits (70%)] -> [Tier 4: Care]`
* **Core Driver Protocol:** Lactic capacity exposure. Hard 4x4 intervals, structured continuous circuits, and linked tracking sets executed with compressed rest profiles.
* **Maintenance Hook Protocol:** Neuro-power defense. The athlete executes 1 or 2 high-intensity, maximum-velocity limit boulder attempts at the absolute start of the session while completely fresh, immediately before moving into the high-fatigue endurance circuits.

### Month 6: Performance & Peak Phase
* **Primary System Trait:** Complete fatigue dissipation and realizations of adaptations.
* **Microcycle Schedule:** 2 Maximum Quality Projecting Sessions, 2 Deep Rest/Mobility Sessions per week.
* **Session Structure:** `[Tier 1: Pulse] -> [Tier 2: Absolute Quality Projecting (100%)] -> [Tier 3: Supercompensation / Rest] -> [Tier 4: Care]`
* **Core Driver Protocol:** Sending execution. Real-world projecting and mock competition style simulations. Systemic training volume drops by 50% while target rest and quality execution parameters hit their absolute peak across the entire macrocycle.

## MONTH 1: CAPILLARY & STRUCTURAL BASELINE

### Phase Structural Rules
* **Weeks 1–3 (Accumulation Blocks):** Steady progression of volume and time-under-tension. Absolute focus on low-intensity, high-density flow to build a dense capillary framework in the forearms.
* **Week 4 (Deload Block):** Automated 45% volume drop across all primary metrics to clear localized toxic waste and allow tissue adaptation before transitioning to Month 2.

### Standardized 40-Minute Prep Container (Days 1, 3, 5)
* **Off-Wall Movement Prep (30 Mins):** Spine Roll (5 Mins), Shoulder band dislocates & wall slides (5 Mins), Core line bird-dogs & planks (5 Mins), Doorway chest stretch (3 Mins), Child's pose & lat tracking (4 Mins), Hip CARs & 90/90 switches (5 Mins), resited dorsiflexion ankle wall-touches (3 Mins).
* **Autoregulated Neural Screening (5 Mins):** Perform 2 submaximal warm-up pulls against a Tindeq portable strain gauge at 50% and 75% MVC (Maximum Voluntary Contraction). If daily force is down by >10% from baseline, reduce Core Driver volume by 50% to prevent connective pulley injury.
* **On-Wall Progressive Ramp (10 Mins):** Easy bouldering focusing on straight-arm skeletal stacking and hip centration on vertical terrain. Zero forearm pump.

### Weeks 1–3 Layout (Month 1: Capillary & Structural Baseline)

#### Day 1: High-Volume Base & ARC Construction (3 Hours)
* **Tier 1: Warm-Up & Prep (40 Mins):** Standard Prep Container with neural screening.
* **Tier 2: Core Driver — Aerobic Regeneration Circuits (ARC) (90 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Continuous on-wall climbing targeting quadrupedal scapular and pelvic engine coordination on vertical to gentle $15^\circ$ overhanging walls.
  * *Execution:* 3 sets $\times$ 20 minutes of continuous climbing. Target a light, highly manageable forearm flush (RPE 4–5) without crossing the anaerobic threshold or building lactic acid.
  * *Constraints:* Rest exactly 10 minutes between sets. Maintain an "engaged rest" (centrated glenohumeral joints) when shaking out.
* **Tier 3: Progress Hook — Slab and Dynamic Slab (40 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Intentionally direct center of mass to negative volumes or no-tex holds.
  * *Execution:* Execute 3 sets of 2–3 distinct low-grade commercial problems. On sloped surfaces, stand further from the wall to push perpendicular; on poor friction surfaces, bring feet flush to the wall and externally rotate hips.
  * *Constraints:* Rest exactly 1 minute between sets.
* **Tier 4: Chiba Tore Diagonal Core & Ankle Spring (10 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* 2 sets $\times$ 10 reps Supine Hand-to-Toe Alternating Touch (diagonal fascia core tension), 2 sets $\times$ 8 reps/side Single-Hand Elevated Scapular Push-Up (serratus anterior motor control), and 2 sets $\times$ 30 seconds Weighted Pogo Jumps (ankle tendon spring compliance for smear volume traction).

#### Day 2: Specialized PT Core & Joint Recovery (2 Hours)
* **Aerobic Flush (30 Mins):** Steady-state nasal breathing jogging or cycling ($<140$ BPM) to promote circulation.
* **Climbing-Specific PT Core Container (40 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* 3 sets $\times$ 10 reps of standing cable Pallof presses, side planks (45s/side), Swiss-ball rollouts, and Seated Contra-Lateral Leg Raises (isolating deep hip flexion).
* **Deep Shoulder, Back & Mobility Matrix (50 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Thoracic extensions over a foam roller, passive sleeper stretches, prone unweighted Y-T-W-L shapes, and long child's pose tracks.

#### Day 3: Baseline Functional Hypertrophy & Fingerboard Loading (3 Hours)
* **Tier 1: Warm-Up & Prep (40 Mins):** Standard Prep Container with neural screening.
* **Tier 2: Core Driver — Compound Strength Overload (90 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Progressive multi-joint off-wall resistance tracking.
  * *Execution:* 3 sets $\times$ 8 reps of strict weighted pull-ups (assisted if necessary), dumbbell floor presses (protects joint capsules), barbell rows, and Heels-Elevated Weighted Squats (quadriceps/patellar tendon conditioning for deep high-stepping).
  * *Constraints:* Rest exactly 2 minutes between sets.
* **Tier 3: Progress Hook — Active Overcoming Isometrics (40 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Autoregulated active finger curls against an immovable fixed block (Tindeq gauge).
  * *Execution:* Perform 6 repetitions of 10-second active maximum-effort concentric pulls using a half-crimp grip. Rest 50 seconds between reps. Bypasses the PIP joint shear stress of traditional deadhangs.
* **Tier 4: antagonist Balance & Plyos (10 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Extensor bands, wrist roller rolls, forearm flexor tissue massage, and **Depth Jumps to Max Vertical Vector** (3 sets $\times$ 5 reps; focus on quick ground contact and explosive energy transfer).

#### Day 4: Asynchronous Recovery Selector
* **Track A:** Complete off-load. Active skin filing, moisturizing, and sleep prioritization.
* **Track B:** Re-execute Day 2's deep mobility flow.

#### Day 5: Low-Intensity Volume Accrual (3 Hours)
* **Tier 1: Warm-Up & Prep (40 Mins):** Standard Prep Container with neural screening.
* **Tier 2: Core Driver — Dynamic Coordination Mileage (90 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* High mileage on dynamic comp-style movement lines.
  * *Execution:* Clear 12–15 intermediate-to-advanced commercial bouldering problems across various wall angles. Focus on generating upward momentum from the pelvis and shoulders rather than over-gripping.
  * *Constraints:* Rest exactly 2 minutes between problems.
* **Tier 3: Progress Hook — Quiet Feet Practice (40 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Footwork accuracy, visual tracking, and core-to-toe load transfer.
  * *Execution:* Focus on silent, precise foot placements. Lock your eyes on the hold until your shoe is fully placed and weighted without readjusting.
* **Tier 4: Postural Realignment & Scapular Release (10 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Cat & Camel mobilization (3 Mins), Spine Roll (3 Mins), and passive centrated overhead "Hang Right" deadhangs (4 Mins).

#### Day 6: Structural Girdle & Kinetic Stabilization (90 Mins)
* **Warm-Up & Mobility (15 Mins):** Band pull-aparts and basic glute activation.
* **Rotator Cuff & Scapular Base Grid (25 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Prone unweighted Y-T-W-L positions (3 sets $\times$ 10 reps) paired with wall slides (3 sets $\times$ 10 reps).
* **Lower Body Alignment & Balance (30 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Bodyweight single-leg Romanian deadlifts (3 sets $\times$ 8 reps/leg) paired with slow bodyweight lunges.
* **Anti-Extension Trunk Stability (20 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* 3 sets $\times$ 12 reps of deadbugs and slow planks.

#### Day 7: Absolute Reset Day
* *Protocol:* Full physiological reset. No training parameters tracked.

## MONTH 2: BASIC STRENGTH PHASE

### Phase Structural Rules
* **Week 1 (Baseline Profile Block):** Establishes baseline resistance markers. Board scaling stays sub-maximal ($< \text{RPE } 7.5$) to ensure movement accuracy.
* **Week 2 (Accumulation Block):** Systemic workload expands by ~15%. Weight loads step up, and rest windows compress to scale neuromuscular density.
* **Week 3 (Peak Strain Matrix Block):** Reaches peak force production and set totals. Effort pushes to the absolute threshold of sub-maximal loading ($\text{RPE } 8.5–9$).
* **Week 4 (Decontamination & Deload Window):** Automated volume reduction of 40–50% across all tracking matrices to clear joint heat and allow muscular supercompensation.

### WEEK 1: BASELINE PROFILE BLOCK

#### Day 1: Advanced Board Climbing & Tension Syntax (3 Hours)
* **Tier 1: Warm-Up & Prep (40 Mins):** Standard Prep Container with neural screening.
* **Tier 2: Core Driver — High-Neural Board Climbing (90 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* High-intensity system board sequences on moderate angles ($30–45^\circ$) focusing on full physical tension.
  * *Execution:* Complete 8–10 distinct advanced board climbs. Focus on generating force from the feet/pelvic engine, maintaining direct tension lines, and managing rotational torque (barndooring) on bad holds.
  * *Constraints:* Intensity is sub-maximal but high ($\text{RPE } 8$). **Rest exactly 3 minutes between burns** to allow complete ATP-PC replenishment.
* **Tier 3: Progress Hook — 1% Capillary Booster & Rotational Core (40 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* High-density, low-intensity climbing paired with diagonal core stabilization.
  * *Execution:* Execute continuous ARC bouldering with a strict **3-second hover cue** directly over every foothold before making weight transfers. Pair with supine diagonal hand-to-toe touches to reinforce diagonal slings.
* **Tier 4: Unilateral Scapular control & Ankle Springs (10 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* 3 sets $\times$ 8 reps/side Single-Hand Elevated Scapular Push-Up, 10 deep bench dips, passive centrated "Hang Right" shoulder hangs (45s), and **Weighted Pogo Jumps** – 2 sets $\times$ 30 seconds holding light dumbbells.

#### Day 2: Physical Therapy Core & Recovery (2 Hours)
* **Aerobic Flush (30 Mins):** Continuous steady-state nasal breathing running ($<140 \text{ BPM}$) to force systemic blood flow and clear waste.
* **Climbing-Specific PT Core Container (40 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* 3 sets of: standing cable Pallof presses (12 reps/side), Hardstyle planks (extended to 45s), 12 reps heavy single-arm farmer's carries/side, and Seated Contra-Lateral Leg Raises (hip flexor conditioning).
* **Deep Shoulder, Back & Mobility Matrix (50 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Foam rolling, passive sleeper stretches, prone dumbbell Y-T-W-L arm raises, puppy pose lat stretches, and cross-legged side twists.

#### Day 3: Heavy Compound Strength & Overcoming Finger Pulls (3 Hours)
* **Tier 1: Warm-Up & Prep (40 Mins):** Standard Prep Container with neural screening.
* **Tier 2: Core Driver — Max Off-Wall Compound Overload (90 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Heavy progressive resistance compound strength.
  * *Execution:* 4 sets $\times$ 6 reps of heavy weighted pull-ups (add +15-30 lbs or progress to 4s eccentrics), barbell rows, heavy dumbbell chest floor presses (protects capsules), and strict hanging L-sits.
  * *Constraints:* Rest a full 3 minutes between sets to allow full neurological recruitment.
* **Tier 3: Progress Hook — Overcoming Isometric pulls (40 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Finger strength profiling on a fixed force gauge at 85% MVC.
  * *Execution:* Perform 5 repetitions of a 7-second active maximum-effort half-crimp pull against a fixed block, followed by 53 seconds of rest. Autoregulated load targeting.
* **Tier 4: antagonist Care & Plyometric Extensions (10 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Forearm cross-friction rolling (4 Mins), reverse wrist dumbbell curls (2 Mins), and **Depth Jumps to Max Box jumps** – 3 sets $\times$ 5 repetitions (Rest 90s between sets for explosive power recruitment).

#### Day 4: Asynchronous Recovery Selector
* **Option A (Active Flush):** Re-execute Day 2's deep mobility and core container.
* **Option B (Passive Rest):** Complete off-load. Zero physical stress to maximize skin repair and clear finger pulley inflammation.

#### Day 5: Modern Dynamic Coordination Volumes (3 Hours)
* **Tier 1: Warm-Up & Prep (40 Mins):** Standard Prep Container with neural screening.
* **Tier 2: Core Driver — Comp-Style Coordination Bouldering (90 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Dynamic parkour volumes and coordination dynos.
  * *Execution:* Complete 10–12 comp-style coordination problems focusing on dynamic hip launches, mid-flight balance corrections, and landing foot coordinates.
  * *Constraints:* **Rest exactly 2 minutes between attempts** to maintain structured density.
* **Tier 3: Progress Hook — Under-Pump Coordination repeats (40 Mins):**
  * *Protocol:* Technical precision on overhanging terrain ($15–30^\circ$) under forearm pump.
  * *Execution:* Run 8 flash-grade problems. Force the pelvic engine to remain flush to the wall and maintain silent foot placements on sub-optimal footholds when fatigued.
* **Tier 4: Hamstring & Ankle Restoration (10 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Resisted ankle dorsiflexion, foam rolling, doorway chest releases, and **Extended Single-Leg Bridges (Toes on Edge)** (3 sets $\times$ 8 reps/leg to isolate biceps femoris for heel-hooks).

#### Day 6: Full Body Climbing-Specific PT Gym Session (90 Mins)
* **Warm-Up & Stabilization (15 Mins):** Band pull-aparts, shoulder internal/external rotations, and glute bridges.
* **Rotator Cuff & Scapular Control Grid (25 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Prone Y-T-W-L raises dumbbell-loaded on a flat bench (3 sets $\times$ 10 reps/position) paired with Serratus wall slides using a foam roller (3 sets $\times$ 12 reps) to promote scapular upward rotation.
* **Lower Body Kinetic Drive & Posterior Sling (30 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Pair **Deficit Kettlebell Reverse Lunges** (3 sets $\times$ 8 reps/leg to isolate hip flexion drive) directly with **Kettlebell Floating Romanian Deadlifts** (3 sets $\times$ 10 reps/leg to lock down posterior chain tension under compression).
* **Pushing Balance & Core Anti-Extension (20 Mins):**
  * *Execution:* Single-arm dumbbell floor presses (3 sets $\times$ 10 reps/side) and form-focused ab-wheel rollouts (3 sets $\times$ 8 reps).

#### Day 7: Absolute Rest Day
* **Protocol:** Zero tracking metrics. Absolute skin, connective tissue, and central nervous system reset.
